# Play

Run PerfectElasticCollision in your browser.
The collision is calculated by the original C++ code,
compiled to [WebAssembly](https://webassembly.org/).

- Red and blue globes have equal mass
- White lines: impulses before the collision
- Gray lines: impulses after the collision
- Check 'Demo' to let the angles change by themselves
